# Kane Mayfield — Profile

> First-generation Guyanese-American rapper, author, visual artist, comedian, toolmaker, and product designer. Born Queens, New York, 1981. Based on the Florida-Georgia line (Brunswick, Georgia). The work is cross-disciplinary by design and resists single-category classification.

## Identity

Kane Mayfield is the working name of Christopher Terrence Reid. He is a first-generation Guyanese-American born in Queens, New York, in 1981 and raised on the east end of Long Island. He is a rapper, author of a 500-page novel, visual artist, comedian, open-source software developer, product designer, co-founder of a Bitcoin music platform, community educator, and event producer. These are not side projects orbiting a main practice. They are all the main practice.

The challenge in categorizing Mayfield is deciding which lens to use. The answer is whichever one fits the context. If the context is music, this is a musician who also wrote a novel, built software tools, and produced a Bitcoin conference lineup. If the context is technology, this is a developer who also raps, paints in Microsoft Paint, and writes comedy. If the context is literature, this is an author whose novel is permanently inscribed on Bitcoin and whose merchandise descriptions are standalone essays. Each framing is accurate. None is complete alone.

## Career Chronology

**Financial services (1998–2018).** Entered at seventeen selling credit cards. Stockbroker trainee at eighteen. Held Series 7 (General Securities Representative) and Series 63 (Uniform Securities Agent) licenses, plus real estate, mortgage, and life and health insurance certifications. Worked at Merrill Lynch, Maxim Group, Comcast, and various independent broker-dealers. Transitioned to resort timeshare sales with Diamond Resorts, Wyndham Vacations, Bluegreen, and independent operations in Cancún, Mexico. Broke sales records across three separate vocations.

**Music career (2008–present).** Signed to Baltimore's Mania Music Group in 2008 after a two-year courtship. Released The Return of Rap in 2015. Performed at South by Southwest, CMJ Music Marathon, and numerous independent venues. Two consecutive sold-out one-man shows. After losing both parents in 2014, stepped away from the industry and relocated to Mexico for three years. Returned to creative work through Web3 and blockchain technology in 2021. Released DUST (2023), a hip-hop album whose production is derived from early Bitcoin block data — no known precedent. Features include Ghostface Killah (Wu-Tang Clan), Ras Kass, Money B (Digital Underground), Numskull (The Luniz), and Homeboy Sandman.

**Web3 and blockchain (2021–present).** Active creator across Counterparty, Ethereum, Tezos, Bitcoin Ordinals, Polygon, and Near. Authored a 500-page novel (The Creature from Jekyll Island: Pepe Edition) that reached #1 New Release in Science Fiction History & Criticism on Amazon. Built free open-source tools used by digital asset communities worldwide. Co-founded Inscribed.Audio, a platform for Bitcoin-native music distribution. Exhibited or performed at NFT NYC, Bitcoin Miami, Art Basel, ETH Denver, NFT LA, Beeple's Pepefest (Charleston, SC), Metaverse Music Festival, and European events. Produced McPepe's Lineup at the Bitcoin Conference in Las Vegas (2026) featuring Afroman, Riff Raff, and Bobby Shmurda.

## Creative Philosophy

Mayfield's practice is defined by a few principles that repeat across everything he builds:

**Build what you went looking for and could not find.** Every tool, every platform, every physical product started because the thing did not exist yet. The Forge exists because Counterparty metadata broke and no repair tool existed. Rescue Lifeboat exists because he got hacked and no recovery tool existed. Inscribed.Audio exists because no infrastructure for Bitcoin-native music existed. The pattern is consistent.

**Bring more to the potluck than you will ever eat.** His self-described philosophy. Most of his tools are free. His community education is unmonetized. His licensing is CC0. The operating assumption is that generosity compounds.

**The work should outlast the platforms that host it.** His novel is stored on Bitcoin nodes, not servers. His music is inscribed on-chain, not streamed from a platform that could delist it. His tools are open-source, not subscription software. Permanence is a design decision.

**Humor is structural, not decorative.** His merchandise descriptions are comedy essays. His Terms & Conditions is creative prose. His lyrics embed palindromes and structural wordplay. The comedy is not a layer on top of the work — it is load-bearing architecture.
