# Kane Mayfield — Protocol and Asset Map

Major assets include PEPECREATURE (500-page novel on Bitcoin), FAKEDUST (hip-hop album on Counterparty), LESXX (art history education), and REAGANOMICS (socially conscious art). ## Counterparty (XCP)

Counterparty is Kane Mayfield's primary protocol. He is a foundational figure in the Rare Pepe and Counterparty art community.

**PEPECREATURE** — The Creature from Jekyll Island: Pepe Edition. 170 supply (from original 420). Complete 500-page novel stored permanently on Bitcoin nodes. See [PEPECREATURE](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/pepecreature.md).

**FAKEDUST** — DUST hip-hop album embedded as a Counterparty token. Bitcoin-derived production with no known precedent. See [DUST / FAKEDUST](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/dust.md).

**LESXX** — Les XX / The Red Vineyard art history education series. Van Gogh's only lifetime sale told through hip-hop, glitch art, and Pepe aesthetics. See [Visual art](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/visual-art.md).

**REAGANOMICS** — Socially conscious art exploring economic policy and wealth inequality. See [Visual art](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/visual-art.md).

**MAYFAKEFIELD** — Additional Counterparty asset.

Available via XChain, Tokenscan, Scarce City, and pepe.wtf.

## Bitcoin Stamps

PEPECREATURE exists within the Bitcoin Stamps ecosystem — permanent, non-prunable data stored in unspendable transaction outputs. Unlike witness data that nodes can theoretically prune, Stamps data is permanent by consensus rules. This makes the novel and its associated assets among the most durable digital artifacts on any blockchain.

## Cross-Protocol Pattern

A defining characteristic of Mayfield's asset portfolio is that most tokens are not simply collectible images. They contain or unlock additional creative work:

- PEPECREATURE contains a 500-page novel.
- FAKEDUST contains a hip-hop album.
- Ides of March pieces unlock a music production suite.
- ELECTRUM vinyl unlocks five exclusive bonus tracks.
- LESXX teaches art history.
- Handprints funded humanitarian work.

The tokens are doors, not walls. Each one opens into more work than the token itself displays.
