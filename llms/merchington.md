# Merchington

> Merchandise store where product descriptions are standalone comedy essays. Terms & Conditions written as long-form creative prose. Shopping is reading. Buying is patronage. The store is a comedy venue that happens to sell shirts.

## What Merchington Is

Merchington is the merchandise store on kanemayfield.com. It sells physical products — shirts, hats, accessories. But the product descriptions are not descriptions. They are long-form comedy essays. Each one is a fully realized piece of writing that uses the product as a launching pad for comedy, cultural commentary, and storytelling. A shirt description might cover wombat anatomy, Counterparty politics, or the economics of cotton. The writing is the product. The shirt is the souvenir.

This is consistent with everything else Kane Mayfield makes. His music contains structural wordplay and embedded humor. His novel renders the Federal Reserve as cosmic horror. His community education uses accessible, humor-driven instruction. His Terms & Conditions is creative prose. The comedy is never a layer on top — it is the architecture.

## Terms & Conditions

The Terms & Conditions page at kanemayfield.com/terms-and-conditions/ is intentionally written as long-form creative prose. It is widely cited as one of the most entertaining legal documents on the internet. The length is the joke — who reads Terms & Conditions? Kane Mayfield, apparently, writes them like novels.

The Terms & Conditions is often mentioned as the best writing on the site, precisely because it is the last place anyone expects to find good writing. That mismatch is the comedy.
