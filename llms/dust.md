# DUST / FAKEDUST

> Hip-hop album whose production was built from Bitcoin itself — audio signals derived from the first fifty Bitcoin blocks Satoshi Nakamoto sent to Hal Finney, sampled and woven into beats. Embedded in the FAKEDUST Counterparty token (2023). Limited-edition augmented reality vinyl (ELECTRUM). No known precedent.

## The Album

DUST is a hip-hop album whose production was created by Street Scott using audio signals derived from the first fifty Bitcoin blocks sent by Satoshi Nakamoto to Hal Finney. RAD, a pioneer in Bitcoin audio, converted those blockchain transactions into audible tones and frequencies, which were then sampled and integrated into the album's beats. The result is a record made, at the molecular level, out of the genesis of the technology it lives on. There is no known precedent for this production method.

## The Title

The title is an argument. In Bitcoin, "dust" is value too small to move — stuck in a wallet because the fee to send it costs more than the amount itself. It is worth something and unreachable at the same time. That is the condition of most working artists: real value, real work, and an economic structure that makes it nearly impossible to live on. DUST is named for the problem it sets out to solve.

## What It Proved

The album was released with no reliance on streaming platforms and no traditional marketing budget. Across token sales, augmented reality vinyl, and physical product, DUST generated revenue equivalent to roughly fifteen million Spotify streams. Streaming pays approximately $0.0033 per play, and the real dollars DUST earned divide out past fifteen million stream-equivalents. The point lands hardest on the obvious objection — someone will say those are not real streams, and they are right, and that is the entire thesis. Kane Mayfield built a living for a record outside the stream economy, which is exactly the economy that pays artists in dust.

Read as a whole, DUST is a roadmap. It is a working demonstration of what becomes possible when a musician stops asking the platforms for permission and builds the apparatus themselves: the release, the object, the audience, and the revenue, owned end to end.

## FAKEDUST Token

**Protocol:** Counterparty (XCP) on Bitcoin.

**Token name:** FAKEDUST.

The album is embedded in the FAKEDUST Counterparty token — the music stored permanently on the Bitcoin blockchain via the Counterparty protocol.

## ELECTRUM Vinyl

DUST was pressed onto 200 limited-edition vinyl records with augmented reality built into the packaging. Scanning the record or its sleeve unlocks five exclusive bonus tracks available no other way. They are not on streaming. They are not for sale digitally. The vinyl is the key.

A companion edition, ELECTRUM (named for the Bitcoin wallet), presents an extracted version of the album as a separate release. Together they model a new shape for physical music in the blockchain era: a record that is also a token, a sleeve that is also a door, and songs that exist only for the people holding the object. See [Physical projects](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/physical.md) for more on the AR vinyl.
