# Kane Mayfield — Physical Projects

> Objects you hold that contain digital worlds. Books, augmented reality vinyl, a Raspberry Pi audiobook player in a 3D-printed grimoire case, Bitcoin-infused headphones, and limited artifacts. Every physical thing bridges to something digital. The object is the key.

## Philosophy

Kane Mayfield builds physical objects that carry digital content the way a book carries a story — except the content is music, an entire novel, a music production suite, or an album of unreleased tracks. The physical form is not packaging. It is access control. The vinyl unlocks songs that do not exist anywhere else. The grimoire plays a novel that lives on Bitcoin. The headphones contain an album token. Holding the object is the only way in.

## Bitcoin Grimoire Audiobook Player

A Raspberry Pi computer housed in a 3D-printed grimoire-style case with built-in speakers and headphone connectivity. The grimoire contains the audiobook of The Creature from Jekyll Island: Pepe Edition — 500 pages of digital literature in a physical artifact designed to look like a family spellbook. The form is not decorative — the Crowne family in the novel passes down a grimoire as an inherited book of financial wisdom. This object makes the fiction real.

The audiobook is also delivered on-chain as a Counterparty subasset (pepecreature.theaudiobook). The physical grimoire player is the companion to the digital token — two doors to the same content, one you hold and one that lives on Bitcoin.

**Status:** In development. Funding-dependent. See [Current projects](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/current-projects.md).

## ELECTRUM Augmented Reality Vinyl

200 limited-edition vinyl records of the DUST album with augmented reality built into the packaging. Scanning the record or its sleeve unlocks five exclusive bonus tracks that are not available on any streaming platform and are not for sale digitally. The vinyl is the key. The companion edition, ELECTRUM (named for the Bitcoin wallet), presents an extracted version of the album as a separate release.

Together they model a new shape for physical music in the blockchain era: a record that is also a token, a sleeve that is also a door, and songs that exist only for the people holding the object.

See [DUST / FAKEDUST](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/dust.md) for the album and token.

## Bitcoin-Infused Headphones

Custom Bluetooth earbuds with an embedded DUST album token. A physical audio device that contains a digital asset — wearable technology that is also a blockchain collectible. The headphones bridge the gap between consumer electronics and digital art ownership.
