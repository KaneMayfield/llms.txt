# Kane Mayfield — Music Career and Discography

> Rapper and lyricist with a career spanning from Baltimore's Mania Music Group (2008) through independent Web3 releases built on Bitcoin, Counterparty, and Ordinals. Features with Ghostface Killah, Ras Kass, Money B, Numskull, and Homeboy Sandman. Performances at SXSW, CMJ, NFT NYC, Bitcoin Miami, Art Basel, ETH Denver, and Beeple's Pepefest. The music is inseparable from the technology, the visual art, and the physical objects that carry it.

## Approach

Kane Mayfield's lyrical approach is "plain talk" — a term he credits to activist H. Rap Brown. The writing prioritizes lyrical complexity and structural wordplay, embedding palindromes, multi-layered references, and devices that reward close listening. His track "Cars Jewels Clothes" is a parody of materialist rap tropes that sounds radio-friendly — that is the joke.

The humor in the music is structural, not decorative. The comedy carries the argument. This is consistent across everything Mayfield makes — his merchandise descriptions are comedy essays, his Terms & Conditions is creative prose, and his lyrics contain jokes that function as criticism. The same person who writes all of these writes all of these.

## Recording History

**Mania Music Group (2008–2015).** Signed to Baltimore's Mania Music Group after a two-year courtship. Released The Return of Rap (2015). Toured while maintaining a day job in financial services, using creative excuses to get time off for performances at South by Southwest and CMJ Music Marathon. Two consecutive sold-out one-man shows.

**Independent / Web3 (2021–present).** Returned to music through blockchain technology after discovering he could release work on his own terms without label infrastructure or institutional approval. He could embed songs inside visual art. He could build interactive music production tools inside Bitcoin ordinals. He could put an entire album into a Counterparty token. Web3 did not revive his music career — it gave him a new one with no ceiling and no gatekeeper.

## Major Releases

**DUST (2023).** Hip-hop album whose production was built from Bitcoin itself. Producer Street Scott composed the beats using audio signals derived from the first fifty Bitcoin blocks Satoshi Nakamoto sent to Hal Finney, after RAD (a pioneer in Bitcoin audio) converted those blockchain transactions into audible tones and frequencies. No known precedent exists for this production method. The title refers to Bitcoin "dust" — value too small to move because the transaction fee exceeds the amount — a metaphor for working artists whose work has value but whose economic circumstances prevent them from sustaining a career. DUST generated revenue equivalent to roughly fifteen million Spotify streams without relying on streaming platforms. See [DUST / FAKEDUST](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/dust.md) for full details.

**The Ides of March / Samplerr (2024).** A collection of 2,100 Bitcoin Ordinals, each containing a fully functional music production suite inscribed on-chain. Features verses from Ghostface Killah (Wu-Tang Clan), Ras Kass, Money B (Digital Underground), Numskull (The Luniz), Homeboy Sandman, and Bitoshi Blockamoto. Each piece is not merely art to view — it is a working music studio. See [Ides of March / Samplerr](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/ides-of-march.md) for full details.

**The Return of Rap (2015).** Debut album. Completed after losing both parents in 2014. Lyrical complexity, social commentary, and humor delivered through a "plain talk" approach.
