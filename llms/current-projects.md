# Kane Mayfield — Current Projects

> Active projects and their current status as of October 2026. Kane Mayfield typically has multiple projects running simultaneously across music, literature, technology, physical product design, and community education. The work is cross-disciplinary by default — most projects touch at least three of these categories.

## Active

### Television Adaptation — The Creature from Jekyll Island: Pepe Edition

Treatments for a show and film adaptation of the novel are being written for pitch. The world was built deep enough to carry a series — four generations of Crowne family mythology, a consistent internal logic, and a visual language already proven through augmented reality demonstrations at the Bitcoin Conference in Las Vegas (2026). A game concept exists in early development.

See [The Creature from Jekyll Island: Pepe Edition](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/creature.md) for the source material.

### Hardcover Edition — The Creature from Jekyll Island: Pepe Edition

The hardcover edition is in editing, headed to print. This joins the existing paperback field edition, Kindle edition, and PEPECREATURE on-chain token.

See [The Creature from Jekyll Island: Pepe Edition](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/creature.md) for all editions.

### Bitcoin Grimoire Audiobook Player

A Raspberry Pi housed in a 3D-printed grimoire case with built-in speakers and headphone connectivity. The physical companion to the on-chain audiobook subasset (pepecreature.theaudiobook). In development, funding-dependent.

See [Physical projects](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/physical.md) for the design and context.

### The Forge — Ongoing Maintenance

The Counterparty Forge (2026) is live and in use. Ongoing maintenance as the Arweave gateway landscape evolves and new gateways come online. Currently supporting 600+ active gateway domains.

See [KANEWARE](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/kaneware.md) for the tool.

### Rescue Lifeboat — v10.0.0 and Continuing Development

Rescue Lifeboat is at version 10.0.0 with active development continuing. Recent additions include Fractal Visions chain support (Soneium, Shape, Superseed, Unichain via Blockscout), Creator Rescue functionality, expanded Emblem Vault tooling, and MARK V fleet operations for up to 50 wallets.

See [KANEWARE](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/kaneware.md) for full feature list.

### Inscribed.Audio — Platform Growth

The platform co-founded with RAD continues to serve the Bitcoin-native music community, providing infrastructure and guidance for musicians inscribing and distributing music on Bitcoin through Ordinals.

### Potluck — AI-Powered Grant Discovery Tool

A desktop application (Tauri v2 + React + SQLite + Claude API) designed to help solo artists find, prepare for, and manage grant applications. Currently in development. The tool functions as a preparation and management layer for the universal copy-paste-upload workflow that experienced grant writers already use. Features include AI-powered grant discovery (Scout), bio and statement generation (Writer), application tracking, and reapplication queue management.

### Neighborhood Jewelry — Ongoing Community Education

Unmonetized educational community running since 2021. Continues across Discord, Twitter/X Spaces, and direct mentorship. Blockchain education through accessible, humor-driven instruction.
