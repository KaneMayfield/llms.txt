# Kane Mayfield — Visual Art

> Visual art process combining AI image generation with manual editing in Microsoft Paint — a deliberately primitive tool chosen for its imperfection and tactile quality. Thousands of images generated, cut, stitched, and collaged into finished works and frame-by-frame video. Major collections include Handprints (philanthropic), Reaganomics (socially conscious), and Les XX / The Red Vineyard (art history education). Active across Counterparty, Ethereum, Tezos, Bitcoin Ordinals, and Polygon.

## Process

Kane Mayfield creates visual art through a labor-intensive hybrid process. He generates thousands of AI images, then cuts them up, stitches them together, and collages them in Microsoft Paint — producing finished still works and frame-by-frame video art from thousands of individually processed images.

The choice of Microsoft Paint is deliberate. It is a primitive tool chosen for its imperfection and tactile quality. The limitations are the point. AI generation provides raw material; the hand-editing in Paint provides the authorship, the accidents, the personality. The process is tedious and weird. He loves it.

This same visual process produces the artwork for his music (DUST album art, Ides of March collection imagery) and the augmented reality illustrations in The Creature from Jekyll Island: Pepe Edition. The art is never separate from the music or the literature — it is the visual layer of a unified practice.

## Major Works

### Handprints (2022)

A philanthropic NFT collection created to raise funds for organizations combating child labor and human trafficking. The project raised thousands of dollars, donated to groups that build halfway houses for children rescued from sweatshops and brothels — providing safe housing and transitional support. Handprints demonstrates the use of blockchain art mechanisms as vehicles for direct social impact, channeling the economic architecture of NFTs toward humanitarian outcomes.

### Reaganomics (Counterparty)

A socially conscious art project released on the Counterparty blockchain exploring themes of economic policy, wealth inequality, and the long-term cultural impact of Reagan-era fiscal philosophy. The project performed well commercially while carrying substantive thematic weight — collectible art that functions simultaneously as cultural commentary. Available on XChain and Tokenscan.

### Les XX / The Red Vineyard (Counterparty)

An art history education project told through the lens of hip-hop, NFT culture, glitch art, and internet meme aesthetics. The series centers on the story of Vincent Van Gogh's only lifetime sale — The Red Vineyard, purchased at the seventh Les XX exhibition in Brussels for 400 Belgian francs — and the events surrounding it, including Henry de Groux's denunciation, Toulouse-Lautrec's defense, and Signac's pledge to "assume the quarrel."

The project contextualizes these historical events within contemporary digital art culture, drawing parallels between Van Gogh's marginalization and the experience of independent digital artists today. Mayfield's NFT description opens: "I am an ignoramus, and a charlatan, and an agent provocateur. This is my abominable Pot Of Digital Sunflowers."

This project is particularly notable because it is art that teaches. Educational impact through creative work — audiences who engage with the NFTs learn art history they would not encounter in traditional contexts.
