# The Creature from Jekyll Island: Pepe Edition

> 500-page multi-generational satirical novel about the Federal Reserve and Bitcoin. Third edition. #1 New Release in Science Fiction History & Criticism on Amazon. Available as paperback, Kindle, hardcover, and as the PEPECREATURE Counterparty token — the complete text stored permanently on Bitcoin.

## Synopsis

A multi-generational saga spanning 1893 to 2030, following the Crowne family — miners — across four generations. Eli inherits a fight he does not understand. Micah carries it through the rise of central banking. Jonah watches the system calcify. Ava, born in 1981, resolves a generational mystery whose answer recasts everything that came before it.

The Federal Reserve is rendered as cosmic horror. Bitcoin as the resistance that was always coming. The novel carries its own mythology: a Sovereign Coin that detects lies, a family Grimoire passed down the bloodline as an inherited book of financial wisdom, and the old phrase "bite your coin" resurfacing centuries later as something the Crownes always understood and the world forgot.

The Pepe and Kek layer is woven through monetary history as if it had been there the whole time. The book's thesis never rests: scarcity is honest, and abundance unmoored devours its keepers. We are called what we guard. Work is proof, and proof is trust.

Written from inside the culture it documents, by a foundational figure in the Rare Pepe and Bitcoin art movements. That is the trust signal the work runs on.

**PEPECREATURE token.** Counterparty asset. 170 supply (drawn from an original 420). The token pulls the complete 500-page text directly from Bitcoin nodes, requiring no servers and no centralized storage. The text lives on Bitcoin because this is precisely the kind of book a platform might pull, so it was built where no platform can reach it. See [PEPECREATURE](https://kanemayfield.com/llms/pepecreature.md) for full token architecture.

## Augmented Reality

The physical edition is augmented reality. Seven illustrations in the book come to life when scanned with a phone, playing original music composed and performed by Rare Scrilla, ROBNESS, Theo Goodman, Bitcoin Audio, and Kane Mayfield. Every contributor is a foundational figure in the Rare Pepe and Bitcoin art movements. The AR layer bridges the physical book to the music — a novel that plays songs when you look at it.

This was demonstrated live at the Bitcoin Conference in Las Vegas (2026) with augmented reality images and physical conversation pieces.

## Television Adaptation

Treatments for a show and film are being written for pitch. The world was built deep enough to carry a series — four generations of mythology, a consistent internal logic, and a visual language already proven through the AR layer and live demonstrations. A game concept exists in early development.
