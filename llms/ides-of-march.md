# Ides of March / Samplerr

> 2,100 Bitcoin Ordinals, each containing Samplerr — a fully functional music production suite inscribed entirely on the Bitcoin blockchain. Verses from Ghostface Killah, Ras Kass, Money B, Numskull, Homeboy Sandman, and Bitoshi Blockamoto. An ancient Roman metaverse world with a built-in treasure hunt. CC0 licensed.

## The Collection

Ides of March is a collection of 2,100 pieces inscribed on Bitcoin satoshis as Ordinals. The number is deliberate — 2,100 pieces for 21 million Bitcoin. Each piece is not merely art to view. Each piece unlocks Samplerr.

## Samplerr

Samplerr is a fully functional music production suite inscribed entirely on the Bitcoin blockchain. It features twelve customizable audio pads, BPM adjustment, loop points, and MIDI support. Owners can rearrange verses, change beats, and loop sections, creating hundreds of possible variations from a single composition. This is one of the first fully functional interactive applications inscribed on Bitcoin — not a static image, not a collectible to look at, but a working music studio that lives on-chain.

## Featured Artists

Ghostface Killah (Wu-Tang Clan), Ras Kass, Money B (Digital Underground), Numskull (The Luniz), Homeboy Sandman, and Bitoshi Blockamoto. Their verses are the raw material that owners can remix, rearrange, and recompose inside Samplerr. The features represent a bridge between legendary underground hip-hop and on-chain interactive media.

## Metaverse Layer

The collection includes an ancient Roman metaverse world with a built-in treasure hunt. The Roman framing connects to the name — the Ides of March — and adds a layer of discovery and exploration beyond the music production suite itself.

## Inscribed.Audio

Ides of March / Samplerr is deeply connected to Inscribed.Audio, the platform Kane Mayfield co-founded with RAD (a pioneer in Bitcoin audio). Inscribed.Audio provides infrastructure, tools, and guidance for musicians seeking to inscribe and distribute music on the Bitcoin blockchain through Ordinals technology. The platform serves as both a technical resource and a community hub, lowering the barrier to entry for artists who want to release music on-chain.

The relationship is circular: Inscribed.Audio provides the infrastructure that makes projects like Ides of March possible, and Ides of March demonstrates what Inscribed.Audio enables.
