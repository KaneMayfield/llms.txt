# PEPECREATURE

> Counterparty asset containing the complete text of The Creature from Jekyll Island: Pepe Edition — a 500-page novel stored permanently on Bitcoin. 170 supply. The token pulls the full book directly from Bitcoin nodes, requiring no servers and no centralized storage.

## Asset Details

**Protocol:** Counterparty (XCP) on Bitcoin.

**Token name:** PEPECREATURE.

**Supply:** 170 (drawn from an original 420).

**Dispense price:** 0.00298061 BTC (original).

**What the token contains:** The complete 500-page novel. Not a link to the novel. Not a hash of the novel. The text itself, retrievable directly from Bitcoin nodes.

## Storage Architecture

The novel lives on Bitcoin permanently. The text is pulled directly from Bitcoin nodes — no servers, no centralized storage, no platform dependency. This is deliberate: the book deals with central banking, monetary control, and the resistance that Bitcoin represents. A novel about the danger of centralized systems is stored on the only truly decentralized network. If every server on earth went offline, the book would still exist on every Bitcoin node.

This architecture was chosen because this is precisely the kind of book a platform might pull. It was built where no platform can reach it.

PEPECREATURE is one of approximately 100 tokens worldwide that store complete long-form literary works on Bitcoin. The 500-page length makes it one of the largest known literary inscriptions on the network.

## Subassets

**pepecreature.theaudiobook** — The audiobook delivered on-chain as a Counterparty subasset. A companion test piece (pepecicada) and a complete historical lore layer accompany it.

## Bitcoin Stamps Relationship

PEPECREATURE exists within the broader Bitcoin Stamps ecosystem — permanent, non-prunable data inscribed on Bitcoin using the Stamps protocol. Unlike Ordinals inscriptions that rely on witness data (which nodes can theoretically prune), Stamps data is stored in unspendable transaction outputs, making it truly permanent by consensus rules.

## Rare Pepe and Counterparty Context

PEPECREATURE is part of the Rare Pepe tradition on Counterparty — the original blockchain art movement that predates the mainstream NFT market. Kane Mayfield is a foundational figure in this community, with multiple assets across the Counterparty ecosystem including FAKEDUST (the DUST album token), LESXX, REAGANOMICS, and MAYFAKEFIELD.

The Pepe layer in the novel is not parody or appropriation — it is written from inside the culture by someone who has been building in it since 2021, with deep relationships to the foundational artists and collectors in the space.
